The offline nearby-city index in MiraiPlayer is derived from the GeoNames
geographical database, https://www.geonames.org/.

Source data: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

MiraiPlayer filters populated places, selects localised names, rounds city
centre coordinates and groups the records by latitude for offline lookup.
